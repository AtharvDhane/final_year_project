Chapter 1: Life Processes
- What are life processes?
- Nutrition in plants: Autotrophic nutrition, photosynthesis
  * Equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2
  * Raw materials: Sunlight, water, CO2, chlorophyll
  * Factors affecting photosynthesis: Light intensity, CO2, temperature
  * Heterotrophic nutrition: Saprotrophic, parasitic, holozoic
  * Nutrition in Amoeba, Paramecium, humans
- Respiration:
  * Aerobic respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + ATP (38 molecules)
  * Anaerobic respiration: fermentation in yeast, lactic acid in muscles
  * Human respiratory system: lungs, alveoli, breathing mechanism
  * Exchange of gases: diffusion mechanism
- Transportation:
  * Human circulatory system: heart (4 chambers), arteries, veins, capillaries
  * Blood composition: RBC, WBC, platelets, plasma
  * Blood pressure: systolic (120 mmHg), diastolic (80 mmHg)
  * Transportation in plants: xylem (water), phloem (food)
  * Transpiration: functions, factors affecting
- Excretion:
  * Human excretory system: kidneys, nephron structure and function
  * Dialysis: hemodialysis process
  * Excretion in plants: diffusion, stomata
  * Osmoregulation

Chapter 2: Control and Coordination
- Nervous system: neurons, nerve impulse, reflex arc
  * CNS: Brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla oblongata), spinal cord
  * PNS: somatic, autonomic
- Chemical coordination: hormones
  * Endocrine glands: pituitary, thyroid, adrenal, pancreas, gonads
  * Deficiency diseases: goiter (iodine), diabetes (insulin)
  * Plant hormones: auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins, ethylene, abscisic acid

Chapter 3: Electricity
- Electric current: I = Q/t, unit: Ampere (A)
- Potential difference: V = W/Q, unit: Volt (V)
- Ohm's Law: V = IR
- Resistance: R = ρL/A
  * Factors: length, cross-section area, material, temperature
- Series circuit: Req = R1 + R2 + R3, same current
- Parallel circuit: 1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2, same voltage
- Electric power: P = VI = I²R = V²/R, unit: Watt (W)
- Electric energy: E = P×t, unit: kWh (kilowatt-hour)
- Heating effect: H = I²Rt (Joule's Law)
- Applications: electric heater, fuse, bulb

Chapter 4: Magnetic Effects of Electric Current
- Magnetic field: direction — right-hand thumb rule, Fleming's left-hand rule
- Oersted's experiment: current produces magnetic field
- Solenoid and bar magnet analogy
- Force on current-carrying conductor: F = BIL
- Electric motor: principle — conversion of electrical → mechanical energy
- Electromagnetic induction: Faraday's law
- Electric generator: AC/DC generators, principle
- Domestic electric circuits: earthing, short circuit, overloading

Chapter 5: Light (Refraction)
- Refraction: bending of light at interface
- Snell's Law: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂
- Refractive index: n = c/v = sin i/sin r
- Real and apparent depth
- Total internal reflection: critical angle, optical fiber
- Lenses: convex (converging), concave (diverging)
- Lens formula: 1/v - 1/u = 1/f
- Magnification: m = v/u = h'/h
- Power of lens: P = 1/f (in meters), unit: Diopter (D)
- Human eye: structure, accommodation, defects
  * Myopia (short-sightedness): concave lens
  * Hypermetropia (long-sightedness): convex lens
  * Presbyopia: bifocal lens
- Dispersion of light: prism, rainbow

Chapter 6: Acids, Bases and Salts
- Acid properties: sour taste, turns litmus red, releases H⁺ ions
- Base properties: bitter taste, turns litmus blue, releases OH⁻ ions
- pH scale: 0-14 (acidic < 7, neutral = 7, basic > 7)
- Neutralization reaction: Acid + Base → Salt + Water
- Important salts: NaCl, Na₂CO₃, NaHCO₃, bleaching powder

Chapter 7: Metals and Non-Metals
- Physical properties: luster, ductility, malleability, conductivity
- Chemical properties: reaction with O₂, water, acids, salts
- Reactivity series: K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > H > Cu > Ag > Au
- Extraction of metals: ore → concentrate → oxide → metal
- Corrosion and prevention: galvanization, anodizing, painting

Chapter 8: Carbon Compounds
- Covalent bonding: sharing of electrons
- Carbon's unique properties: tetravalency, catenation
- Functional groups: alcohols (-OH), aldehydes (-CHO), ketones (-CO-), carboxylic acids (-COOH)
- Homologous series: CnH2n+2 (alkanes), CnH2n (alkenes), CnH2n-2 (alkynes)
- Ethanol: properties, uses, denatured alcohol
- Ethanoic acid (acetic acid): properties, ester formation

Chapter 9: Heredity and Evolution
- Mendel's laws of inheritance: dominance, segregation, independent assortment
- Monohybrid cross: Aa × Aa → 1AA:2Aa:1aa (3:1 phenotypic ratio)
- Dihybrid cross: 9:3:3:1 ratio
- Sex determination: XX (female), XY (male)
- Evolution: Darwin's theory, natural selection, survival of the fittest
- Speciation: geographic isolation, reproductive isolation
- Evidence of evolution: homologous organs, analogous organs, fossils

Chapter 10: Our Environment
- Ecosystem: biotic and abiotic components
- Food chain and food web
- Trophic levels and energy flow (10% rule)
- Biodegradable vs non-biodegradable waste
- Ozone layer depletion: CFCs, UV radiation effects
- Greenhouse effect: CO₂, CH₄, N₂O → global warming
