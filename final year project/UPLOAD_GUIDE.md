# 📚 Document Upload Guide & Use Cases

This guide explains what kind of files and documents should be uploaded by each type of user (Student, Teacher, Researcher) in the **Smart Research & Learning Assistant**. It also explains *why* they should upload these files and what AI features they can unlock.

Save this guide for later reference when demonstrating the project or preparing test data!

---

## 🎓 1. For Students (`ROLE_STUDENT`)

**Goal:** To understand complex topics quickly, prepare for exams, and retain knowledge.

### 📄 What to Upload:
- **Textbooks (PDF):** Full course books, reference materials, or specific chapters.
- **Lecture Slides (PPTX or PDF):** Presentations provided by the professor.
- **Class Notes (DOCX or TXT):** Typed notes from class lectures.
- **Research Articles (PDF):** Supplementary reading materials given as assignments.

### 🚀 Why upload these? (Features unlocked):
- **AI Summarizer:** Instantly get an executive summary of a 100-page textbook chapter.
- **Study Notes Generator:** Convert messy class notes or dense books into perfectly formatted, structured markdown notes.
- **Practice MCQ Quiz:** Upload a textbook chapter and instantly generate 5 practice questions to test your knowledge before an exam.
- **Active Recall Flashcards:** Upload lecture slides to automatically create a 3D flip-card deck for memorization.
- **ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5):** If a concept in the textbook is too hard, ask the AI to simplify it.
- **Multi-Language Translation:** Translate foreign study materials into Hindi, Spanish, etc.

---

## 👨‍🏫 2. For Teachers (`ROLE_TEACHER`)

**Goal:** To reduce administrative workload, create engaging lesson plans, and evaluate student progress.

### 📄 What to Upload:
- **Syllabus / Curriculum (PDF or DOCX):** The official course outline for the semester.
- **Past Question Papers (PDF):** Previous years' exams.
- **Reference Books & Research Papers (PDF):** High-level material to incorporate into lessons.
- **Student Assignments/Answers (DOCX or TXT):** Submissions from students for grading.

### 🚀 Why upload these? (Features unlocked):
- **Question Paper Generator:** Upload the syllabus and let the AI automatically generate a balanced exam paper.
- **Lesson Plan Creator:** Upload a reference book chapter, and the AI will structure a 45-minute lesson plan for you.
- **Teaching Material Generator:** Convert dense textbooks into easy-to-understand student handouts.
- **Performance Analyzer & Evaluation:** Upload student answer sheets to get an AI-assisted evaluation and feedback on where the class is struggling.

---

## 🔬 3. For Researchers (`ROLE_RESEARCHER`)

**Goal:** To analyze vast amounts of literature, find research gaps, and organize citations.

### 📄 What to Upload:
- **Academic Papers & Journals (PDF):** IEEE, Springer, or Elsevier publications related to their domain.
- **Literature Reviews (DOCX):** Existing reviews of the field.
- **Conference Proceedings (PDF/PPTX):** Presentations and papers from academic conferences.
- **Data Reports / Transcripts (TXT / DOCX):** Qualitative data or interview transcripts.

### 🚀 Why upload these? (Features unlocked):
- **Paper Analyzer:** Upload a 30-page research paper and instantly get the methodology, results, and limitations summarized.
- **Literature Review Generator:** Upload multiple papers and let the AI synthesize them into a cohesive literature review.
- **Gap Finder:** The AI will analyze the uploaded papers and identify what is missing or what future research can be done (crucial for PhDs).
- **Comparison Tool:** Upload two different papers (e.g., two different machine learning algorithms) and have the AI compare their methodologies and outcomes.
- **Citation Extraction:** Automatically find and format citations from the uploaded documents so you don't have to manually type them out.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for the Best AI Results
1. **Quality Matters:** Upload PDFs with selectable text (not scanned images/photos), as the AI can read them much faster and more accurately.
2. **Chunking:** If a textbook is 1000 pages, try splitting it into smaller chapters (e.g., 50-100 pages) before uploading. This gives the AI better focus.
3. **Formats Supported:** `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.pptx`, and `.txt`.
